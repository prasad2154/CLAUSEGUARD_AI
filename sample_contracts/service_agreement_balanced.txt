SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025, by and between:

Acme Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation, with its principal place of business at
1234 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA 94105 ("Client")

and

TechVendor Solutions LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 Services. Vendor shall provide software development and IT consulting services
as set forth in each Statement of Work ("SOW") mutually agreed upon and executed by
the parties from time to time. Each SOW is hereby incorporated by reference into
this Agreement.

1.2 Changes. Any changes to a SOW must be agreed upon in writing by both parties
before implementation. Changes may result in additional fees or extensions of time.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. TERM AND TERMINATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a
period of twelve (12) months, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this
Agreement ("Initial Term"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew
for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of
non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.2 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement or any
SOW for any reason or no reason upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the
other party.

2.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may immediately terminate this Agreement
upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails
to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days after receiving written notice of such
breach.

2.4 Effect of Termination. Upon termination or expiration, Vendor shall promptly
deliver to Client all completed work product and any work in progress. Client shall
pay for all services rendered and expenses incurred through the effective date of
termination.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. PAYMENT TERMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 Fees. Client agrees to pay Vendor the fees as set forth in each applicable SOW.

3.2 Invoicing. Vendor shall invoice Client on a monthly basis. All invoices are due
and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3.3 Late Payments. Invoices not paid within thirty (30) days shall accrue interest
at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate
permitted by applicable law, whichever is less.

3.4 Expenses. Client shall reimburse Vendor for all reasonable out-of-pocket expenses
incurred in connection with the Services, provided such expenses are approved in
writing by Client in advance and documented with appropriate receipts.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 Work Product Ownership. All work product, inventions, developments, improvements,
and other deliverables created by Vendor specifically for Client under this Agreement
("Work Product") shall be deemed works made for hire and shall be owned solely by
Client. To the extent any Work Product does not constitute a work made for hire,
Vendor hereby assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in such Work Product.

4.2 Pre-Existing IP. Vendor retains all rights in its pre-existing tools, methodologies,
frameworks, and intellectual property ("Vendor IP"). Vendor grants Client a non-exclusive,
perpetual, royalty-free license to use any Vendor IP incorporated into the Work Product
solely to the extent necessary to use the Work Product.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. CONFIDENTIALITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1 Confidential Information. Each party ("Receiving Party") may receive or have
access to confidential or proprietary information of the other party ("Disclosing
Party") ("Confidential Information"). Confidential Information includes, without
limitation, business plans, technical data, financial information, and customer lists.

5.2 Non-Disclosure. The Receiving Party agrees to: (a) hold Confidential Information
in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information only for purposes of performing
obligations under this Agreement; and (c) disclose Confidential Information only to
those employees or contractors who need to know such information and are bound by
confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those herein.

5.3 Duration. These confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this
Agreement for a period of three (3) years; provided, however, that obligations with
respect to trade secrets shall continue for so long as such information constitutes
a trade secret under applicable law.

6.2 Liability Cap. EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY TO THE OTHER PARTY FOR ALL CLAIMS

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

9.1 Negotiation. In the event of any dispute, the parties shall first attempt to
resolve the dispute through good-faith negotiation between senior representatives of
